How We Define Completion

Day 1: agree the baseline.
Day 30: verify the handoff.

At kickoff, we confirm the initiative boundary, available evidence, participants, and acceptance criteria. At handoff, leadership reviews the agreed deliverables against these conditions:

Risks are visiblePriority risks and blockers are documented with evidence, status, and next actions.
Ownership is explicitAccountable owners and escalation paths are recorded; unresolved ownership decisions are clearly flagged.
Decisions have structureOutstanding decisions identify the responsible decision-maker, evidence needed, and applicable stage-gate criteria.
Leadership has a path forwardThe executive brief records current state, prioritized actions, outstanding decisions, and the governance cadence for ongoing use.

Completion criteria and timing assume timely access to the agreed information and stakeholder participation. Material changes are reviewed together.

AI Business Impact & Secure Implementation

AI adoption is a business decision.
Understand the impact before you scale.

Putting AI into a workflow does not, by itself, establish business value. Leaders need to understand what changes, what information is exposed, where human judgment remains essential, and whether the solution is practical for their teams.

“Will this AI solution help our business—and what new risks or dependencies will it create?”

Veriscope brings a cybersecurity-centered perspective to AI adoption. We help you assess the business impact, identify vulnerabilities and operational challenges, and implement an appropriate solution with safeguards matched to your use case.

For planned or existing AI use. Start with a proposed use case, an active pilot, or tools already used across the business. We examine the intended benefit alongside data handling, access, output reliability, vendor dependencies, and accountability.

Value must be demonstrated. Agree a baseline and success measures—such as time saved, output quality, rework, cost, or decision turnaround—and assess pilot results before expanding adoption.
